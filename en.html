FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What does Agency Damah do?
Agency Damah is a Seoul-based promotion agency specializing in popup store operations, professional promoter and event staff recruitment, brand activations, and All-in-One offline event management. With 3,500+ events completed, we have worked with major brands including LG, KREAM, Musinsa, and more.
Can international brands use Agency Damah for events in Korea?
Yes. Agency Damah regularly partners with international brands entering the Korean market. We handle everything from concept to execution — staffing, brand education, on-site management, and post-event reporting. Contact us at damah@agencydamah.com.
What types of events do you specialize in?
We specialize in popup stores, flagship store operations, trade fairs, product launches, K-beauty activations, food and beverage promotions, health supplement campaigns, baby and kids fairs, camping and lifestyle events, and corporate brand experiences across Korea.
How do I get a quote?
Email us at damah@agencydamah.com or use the KakaoTalk chat button on this page. We provide customized quotes based on your event type, duration, and staffing requirements — always transparent and budget-friendly.
Where is Agency Damah located?
Our office is located in Mapo-gu, Seoul (Wausan-ro 30-gil 135). We operate events nationwide across Korea. Office hours are Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM KST.
